TriggerTrack Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

Local data

TriggerTrack stores your diary entries on this device. It does not create an account or upload health data.

Deletion

Deleting the app or clearing app data removes the entries. You can export and import JSON backups to move or restore your diary.

Tracking

TriggerTrack uses no analytics, advertising, account services, or tracking SDKs.

Feedback

Feedback is opened in the user’s own email app. Do not include health details.

Medical use

TriggerTrack is a personal tracking tool. Insights are clues, not proof or medical diagnosis or advice.
